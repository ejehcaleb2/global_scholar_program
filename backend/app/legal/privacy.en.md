# Privacy policy — owner drafting skeleton

## Responsible organization

State the legal identity, address and contact details of the organization responsible for this program and its data.

## Scope and eligibility

State who may use the service, eligibility rules, and the scope of these terms.

## Data collected

List the actual account, profile, learning, consent and service-request data collected.

## Purposes and legal basis

State each actual purpose and its applicable legal basis after qualified review.

## Retention

State justified retention periods and deletion practices for each data category.

## Sharing and processors

Name the actual recipients, processors, hosting locations and any cross-border safeguards.

## Minors and guardians

State the reviewed rules for minors, guardians and age-appropriate information.

## Uploaded documents

Explain the actual handling, permitted use, access controls and retention of uploaded documents.

## Rights and requests

State applicable rights and the actual process and contact for exercising them.

## Security

Describe the actual safeguards without making unsupported guarantees.

## Use of the program

State the actual user obligations, content rights and service limitations.

## Contact and complaints

Provide the real contact channel and reviewed complaints process.

## Changes

State how material changes, versioning and renewed acceptance will be communicated.
